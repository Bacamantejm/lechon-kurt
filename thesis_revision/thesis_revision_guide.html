4. Capstone Defense Oral Examination Quick-Reference

Anticipated questions from the panel of examiners (Jeremy Lloyd N. Claveria, Marie Joy C. Ignacio, Ebreo D. Blasurca, and Dr. Marife Grace D. Gagalac) along with recommended technical defense answers grounded in your codebase:

Question 1: "How does the Decision Support System (DSS) specifically help pork lechon shop owners make decisions?"

Defense Answer: "The DSS (implemented in admin/forecasting_dashboard.php and admin/dss_reports.php) analyzes past order volume and product-level sales using time-series analysis (Single Exponential Smoothing with smoothing constant α = 0.3). In addition, because Cavite lechon demand surges during municipal town fiestas and holidays, the system includes a business_events multiplier module. It combines historical trend lines with event coefficients to project the exact number of whole pigs and kilos of belly needed, automatically warning the owner if current raw material inventory in mrp.php falls below safe operating levels."

Question 2: "How is data isolated between different competing lechon shops in Cavite on one platform?"

Defense Answer: "The platform uses multi-tenant data scoping enforced in admin/auth.php. Each registered store owner has a unique record in store_locations linked to their owner_user_id. Whenever an admin views orders, inventory, or expenses, the backend queries automatically append WHERE p.seller_id = ? or filter by pickup_location. Shop A can never view or modify the sales, customer reviews, or inventory of Shop B."

Question 3: "Why did you replace customer PIN verification with photo proof of delivery?"

Defense Answer: "During initial field testing with delivery riders, we observed that requiring a 4-digit PIN caused handover friction, particularly when customers sent relatives or household staff to receive large whole lechons at the gate without knowing the order PIN. Replacing the PIN with photo proof of delivery (captured through rider/active_delivery.php) mirrors commercial standards (GrabFood/Foodpanda). It provides indisputable visual proof of handover, stores geotagged image verification in uploads/proof_of_delivery/, and notifies both the customer and store owner immediately."

Question 4: "Is the mobile application a native app or web-based?"

Defense Answer: "The system utilizes a responsive web-based progressive mobile architecture with dedicated mobile-first portals for customers (index.php, track_order.php) and delivery couriers (rider/index.php, rider/active_delivery.php). It provides mobile accessibility without requiring high-maintenance cross-platform compilation, utilizing HTML5 Geolocation API, responsive layouts, and asynchronous REST polling."
