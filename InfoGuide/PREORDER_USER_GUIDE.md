# 🎯 Multi-Step Pre-Order System - Quick Start Guide

## What's New? 

Your pre-ordering experience has been completely redesigned with:
- **5-Step Wizard** - Easy to follow, one step at a time
- **Google Maps** - Auto-complete addresses and capture exact location
- **Time Slots** - Choose from 8 AM to 9 PM in hourly slots
- **Beautiful Alerts** - Modern SweetAlert2 confirmations
- **Real-Time Updates** - See your total price as you select options

---

## 📍 Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1️⃣ - Select Your Product
1. Browse available lechon products
2. Choose your favorite
3. Use **+** and **−** buttons to set quantity
4. See instant price calculation
5. Click **Next →**

**What You'll See:**
- Product images and prices
- Quantity selector
- Running subtotal
- Green checkmark when product is selected

---

### Step 2️⃣ - Choose Delivery Method

#### Option A: Pickup from Store 🏪
1. Select "Pickup" option
2. Browse available store locations
3. Click your preferred store
4. Click **Next →**

**What You'll See:**
- Store name and address
- Green highlight when selected
- No delivery fee

#### Option B: Home Delivery 🏠
1. Select "Delivery" option
2. Type your address in the search box
3. Google Maps will suggest addresses
4. Click a suggestion or adjust on the map
5. Your coordinates are automatically captured
6. Click **Next →**

**What You'll See:**
- Address search box
- Interactive map showing your location
- Latitude and Longitude (auto-filled)
- ₱150 delivery fee

---

### Step 3️⃣ - Select Date & Time

1. **Pick a Date:**
   - Click the calendar input
   - Select a future date (no past dates allowed)
   - Only available dates will be selectable

2. **Pick a Time:**
   - Choose from 14 hourly slots
   - Available: **8:00 AM to 9:00 PM**
   - Click on your preferred time
   - It will turn red when selected

**Available Time Slots:**
```
Morning:   8 AM  | 9 AM  | 10 AM | 11 AM | 12 PM
Afternoon: 1 PM  | 2 PM  | 3 PM  | 4 PM  | 5 PM
Evening:   6 PM  | 7 PM  | 8 PM  | 9 PM
```

Click **Next →**

---

### Step 4️⃣ - Choose Payment Option

#### Option A: 30% Downpayment (Recommended) 💳
- Pay only 30% now
- Pay remaining 70% at pickup/delivery
- Best for trying before full commitment

#### Option B: Full Payment 💰
- Pay everything now
- Nothing left to pay at pickup/delivery
- Completes transaction immediately

**See Your Breakdown:**
- Subtotal
- Delivery Fee (if applicable)
- Total Amount
- Downpayment amount
- Remaining balance

Click **Next →**

---

### Step 5️⃣ - Review & Confirm

This is your final check:
- ✅ Product name and quantity
- ✅ Delivery method and location
- ✅ Date and time selected
- ✅ All prices and breakdown
- (Optional) Add special instructions

Click **"Proceed to Payment"** button to:
1. Go to PayMongo payment gateway
2. Select your payment method (Card, GCash, Maya, etc.)
3. Complete payment
4. Receive confirmation email

---

## 🎨 UI Tips & Tricks

### Understanding the Colors
- **Red** (#c62828) = Selected/Active item
- **Green** = Completed step
- **Gray** = Not yet selected
- **Blue outline** = Form focus (hover over input)

### Navigation
- **Next Button** (Red) - Moves forward
- **Back Button** (Gray) - Go to previous step
- **Progress Bar** - Shows where you are (Steps 1-5)
- **Numbers** - Current step circled in red

### Real-Time Help
- Don't have all info? Come back later (not saved automatically)
- Missing something? Red alert will tell you what's needed
- Prices update instantly as you change options

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I save my order and come back later?**
A: Not yet - complete the order in one session. Your cart items are saved separately.

**Q: What if I choose the wrong time?**
A: Just click the "Back" button to go to Step 3 and select a different time.

**Q: Do I need to enter my exact address?**
A: For delivery, yes. Type and let Google Maps auto-complete. It captures your coordinates.

**Q: Which payment method is best?**
A: If unsure, choose 30% Downpayment. You only commit to 30% immediately.

**Q: Can I add special requests?**
A: Yes! In Step 5, add notes like "No onions" or "Extra sauce" in Special Instructions.

**Q: What time slots are available?**
A: 8 AM to 9 PM, every hour. Choose what's convenient for you.

**Q: Is delivery available everywhere?**
A: Currently ₱150 in Metro Manila. Other areas coming soon!
